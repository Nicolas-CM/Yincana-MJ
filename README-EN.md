# Campus Adventure Hunt

Welcome to **Campus Adventure Hunt**! This is a fun, interactive web app designed for students to explore their campus, complete creative tasks, and work together as a team.

## How to Play

1. **Pick Your Team:**
   - Go to the "Teams" tab and select an existing team or create a new one.

2. **Complete the Tasks:**
   - Go to the "Map" tab. There are 5 tasks to complete, each in a different location on campus.
   - Tasks must be completed in order. Finish one to unlock the next.

3. **Take Photos:**
   - For each task, take a photo as proof of completion. You can upload or capture photos directly in the app.

4. **Win and Celebrate:**
   - After finishing all 5 tasks, your team will be congratulated! Return to your classroom and show your photos to your teacher.

## Features

- Mobile-friendly, easy-to-use interface
- Team selection and creation
- Interactive campus map with progress tracking
- Photo upload for each challenge
- Fun, colorful design

## Technologies Used

- HTML, CSS, JavaScript (Vanilla)
- No backend required (all data is stored in memory)

## Getting Started

1. Open `campus_adventure_hunt.html` in your web browser (preferably on a mobile device for the best experience).
2. Follow the instructions on the screen to start your adventure!

## Customization

- You can edit the tasks, team names, and map design by modifying the `campus_adventure_hunt.html` file.
